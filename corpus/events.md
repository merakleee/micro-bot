AGC is an annual event that aims to bring together game developers through a game development competition and series of activities such as workshops, conferences and trainings. This event originally named the 'XNA' game challenge was created in 2011 under the partnership of Microsoft, referring to their XNA game engine. Later, the event was renamed Algeria Game Challenge in its 3rd edition in 2013, the competition has been held every year for a total of 9 successful editions.

Micro Club Capture The Flag is an event that reflects all sides of cybersecurity, gathering all the interested people in one competition to exchange knowledge and experience. this event was held in two parts : The qualifications, which were held completely online, where participants solved challenges to gain points and be qualified for the finale. and the Finale where we delivered a lot of Cyber Security content.
