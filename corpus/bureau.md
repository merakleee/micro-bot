Executive Board Election
The Executive Board is the highest governing authority of MicroClub. It consists of four
members: President, Vice President, Secretary General, and Deputy Secretary General, all
elected by the club membership. At the end of each season, the club conducts internal online
elections to select the new Executive Board.
Introduction
The election of the Executive Board follows a two-phase process: an internal online election
followed by a University General Assembly. At the university level, the Bureau consists of seven
members: the three principal executives (President, Vice President, and Secretary General) and
the four Substitute members, the first of whom is the Deputy Secretary General. All seven
hold the same authority and standing. Internally, the responsibilities and tasks of Substitute
members may vary as determined by the new Executive Board.
Eligibility
Candidacy for any Executive Board position is governed by the following criteria. All candidates
must satisfy every condition listed below at the time of nomination and throughout the electoral
process.
1. USTHB Enrollment: Candidates must be currently enrolled students at the University of
Science and Technology Houari Boumediene.
2. Faculty Restriction: Candidates for the positions of President and Vice President must
be enrolled in the Faculty of Computer Science.
3. Membership Duration: Candidates must have been members of MicroClub for a minimum
of one full year, from the beginning to the end of that period, at the time of nomination.
4. Active Participation: Candidates must have been active during the current season, with
consistent engagement in club activities and events.
5. Responsibility and Commitment: A candidate who has disappeared or withdrawn from
club activities without a valid reason, abandoned important assigned tasks, or failed to take
their responsibilities seriously is ineligible to stand for election.
6. Experience Requirement: Candidates must satisfy at least one of the following:
– Held at least one formal responsibility or role within the club.
– Participated in the organization of at least two major events, primarily of an external
or public-facing nature.
7. Clean Disciplinary Record: Candidates must have no active (non-archived) formal warnings
on their disciplinary record at the time of nomination (see Article 25, Warning Archival).

Electoral Process
The electoral process follows a structured sequence of stages defined by the current Executive
Board in coordination with IR-HR. The timeline for each stage is set according to the club’s
seasonal agenda. Voting is conducted online by secret ballot and proceeds position by position.
1. Election Announcement: The current Executive Board shall formally announce the opening
of the electoral period, specifying the timeline, available positions, and eligibility requirements.
2. Pre-Election: Candidates shall submit their applications through the official election form
within the designated period. All applications shall be reviewed to verify that each candidate
meets the eligibility criteria set forth in this chapter. Candidates who do not satisfy the
criteria shall be disqualified, with the justification communicated to them in writing.
3. Nominations: Approved candidates shall formally declare the position sought. A candidate
may stand for the position of President and, if not elected, may stand for any of the remaining
positions. This intent must be specified at the time of nomination.
4. Campaign and Presentations: Approved candidates shall campaign and present their
programs and vision for the club to the membership within the designated campaign period.
Campaigning must remain respectful, constructive, and focused on the candidate’s qualifications.
The following conduct is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate disqualification:
– Negative campaigning, personal attacks, or defamation directed at other candidates.
– The offering of incentives, favors, or promises of positions in exchange for votes.
– Campaigning or promotion outside the official MicroClub Discord server.
– Using the capabilities, authority, or influence of a held position within the club for
campaign promotion.
– Campaigning outside the designated period or through unauthorized channels.
5. Voting: Voting shall be conducted online by secret ballot. All active members are entitled
to cast one vote per position. Voting proceeds position by position in the following order:
President, Vice President, Secretary General, Deputy Secretary General.
Voting is weighted as follows:
– Members of the current Executive Board hold a vote weight of four (4).
– Active former Executive Board members (former executives who remain involved in club
activities) hold a vote weight of two (2).
– All other members hold a vote weight of one (1).
6. Tie Votes: In the event of a strict tie for any position, the vote shall be retaken among the
tied candidates. The process shall be repeated until the tie is resolved.
7. Announcement of Results: The results shall be announced by the current Executive
Board and IR-HR upon completion of the vote count and verification.
8. Transition and University General Assembly: The period between the internal election
and the University General Assembly constitutes the transition period. At the General
Assembly, members formally vote for the elected President. The President then chooses the
elected Vice President, Secretary General, and Deputy Secretary General.

President then chooses the agreed-upon remaining Substitute members, ordered as second,
third, and fourth Substitute. All Substitute members must meet the same eligibility criteria
as the elected executives.
