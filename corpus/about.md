Definition: MicroClub, founded on March 5, 1985, is Algeria’s first student science club, located at USTHB’s Computer Science Faculty.
I.2. Mission: As a non-profit organization, its mission is to promote the development of computer science and volunteerism through workshops, training sessions, projects, and events. 
I.3. Code of Conduct: Written by the Executive Board and approved and validated by the IR-HR department, this administratively approved document is designed to explain the internal operations and common practices of the club to its members. Although not an exhaustive list of rules, it serves as a guide to ensure smooth organization and prevent misunderstandings. The code specifically aims to:
 – Ensure smooth organization and operational efficiency within the club.
 – Prevent conflicts and resolve misunderstandings among the membership.

Members are required to respect these directives. Penalties for violations range from formal warnings to potential expulsion (kicking) or permanent banning. In cases where an offense is deemed severe or illegal, the matter will be reported to the University Administration for further academic or legal action. Members are expected to stay informed and well aware of all contents herein.

II.1. Hierarchy and Authority: MicroClub operates through a structured chain of command. Members must respect the authority of department and section leads, Internal Relations & Human Resources department, and the Executive Board. 
  
II.2. Language Policy: All club operations and communications are conducted in English, the global standard for computer science. Members are encouraged to improve their English proficiency for academic and professional growth.
  
II.3. Official Communication Platform: Discord is the mandatory primary platform for MicroClub. Being present on the server is a requirement for active membership. Furthermore, official decisions and announcements flow through designated channels on the server to ensure transparency and accountability.
